GODOT ENGINE / C# / 3D

ABOUT

Sanatorium is the intersection of botanical horror, science, and technology. It revolves around an institution that attempted to turn an extraordinary discovery into something revolutionary, only to lose control of its own ambitions. The Indigo flower is the foundation of this universe, its influence spreads through anomalies, experimental machinery, and the institution's research.


FEATURES

Flip Phone

One of the game's central mechanics is a specialized flip phone capable of photographing manifestations of the flower. These photographs can be translated into signals that experimental machinery uses to produce botanical samples.

Horror & Survival

Heavily inspired in low-poly horror games, the game focuses on the atmosphere and uncertainty. Instead of a single enemy chasing the player, the player needs to avoid mistakes while exploring the sanatorium to stay alive.
